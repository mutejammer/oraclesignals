// KEY SECURITY

Your key can't move your money out

This is the part that matters most, so here it is in plain terms.

▣ API keys cannot withdraw

A Kalshi API key can read your account and place trades — nothing else. Withdrawals can only happen inside your own Kalshi login, with your own credentials. There is no API endpoint that sends your money anywhere, so a leaked key could never drain your balance to someone else's account.

▣ Encrypted at rest

Your key is encrypted before it's stored and only decrypted in memory at the moment a trade is placed. It is never shown back in full, never emailed, and never logged.

■ The kill switch

Every account gets a big pause button in the terminal — one tap halts all trading on your account instantly — plus a daily loss cap you set yourself. Hit the cap and ORACLE stops for the day on its own. You're always one tap from stopping it.

▣ One Discord, one Kalshi

One Discord account links to exactly one Kalshi account. If you relink to a different Kalshi account, there's a 30-day cooldown before you can relink again — free weeks and free days can't be passed around or farmed across accounts.

▣ Revoke anytime, instantly

Delete the key in your Kalshi settings and ORACLE's access ends that second — no support ticket, no waiting period. You can also pause trading from your terminal with one tap.

▣ Use a dedicated subaccount

Our recommendation: open a separate Kalshi subaccount just for ORACLE, fund it with only what you're comfortable risking, and keep your main balance untouched. Clean separation, hard limit by design.

▲ The honest part

A trading key can't steal your money — but it can lose it trading. ORACLE will have losing windows and losing days; the record above includes them. Your per-trade size and daily cap exist so a bad stretch stays a bad stretch, not a disaster. Never fund it with money you can't afford to lose.

// QUESTIONS

Asked straight, answered straight

Is my money safe?

Your money stays in your own Kalshi account the entire time — ORACLE never holds it. A Kalshi API key cannot withdraw funds; money can only leave your account from inside your own Kalshi login. Your key is stored encrypted, and you can revoke it in Kalshi settings at any moment. What no one can promise is trading profit: the bot can and does lose trades, which is why you set the size and the daily cap.

Can I stop anytime?

Yes, three ways: hit the big pause button in your terminal (you're always one tap from stopping it), cancel your subscription (access runs to the end of the current pass), or revoke the API key in Kalshi — that one is instant and total. Your self-set daily loss cap also stops trading automatically when it's hit. Any position already open settles normally in your Kalshi account; it's yours.

What does it trade?

Bitcoin only: Kalshi's 15-minute BTC up/down markets. One asset, one timeframe, one strategy — a multi-timeframe momentum model that cross-checks Kalshi's implied price before it trades, sized by your settings and bounded by your daily cap.

What are sit-outs?

Windows where ORACLE's signals disagree with each other or with Kalshi's price, so it places no trade. About 1 in 4 windows is a sit-out. Skipping the coin-flips is a big part of why the traded record looks the way it does — forcing a trade every window would be worse, and the graded history shows both.

How does the free 1-hour trial work?

It's for everyone, no card required. Create an account and press Start — your 60 minutes begin at that tap, not when you signed up. You get one trial per person, tied to your account and your Kalshi lock, so it can't be restarted or farmed across accounts. It's separate from the Discord-linked claims: members still get their one free day and VIPs still get their monthly free week, and those still require a linked Discord first.

What is the sealed marker?

When ORACLE's decision locks in, a sealed marker is stamped on the live chart at that exact moment — side, time into the window, and entry. After the seal, the call never changes: no edits, no quiet flips. Checkpoint meters then show who's winning at 13, 10, 7, 5 and 3 minutes left, and the window ends with a settlement reveal graded against Kalshi's official result.

How do the free claims work?

Every claim starts with linking your Discord — no linked Discord, no claim. That's how eligibility is validated. Server members get one free day; VIP members get one free 7-day week every month. Both are claim-based: your time starts the moment you tap claim, not before. Free weeks don't roll over or stack. If you're on a paid subscription and claim a free week, your paid clock pauses while the free week runs. And if you lose the VIP role, free VIP access ends immediately — no grace period.

Can I share my access or switch Kalshi accounts?

No sharing: one Discord account links to exactly one Kalshi account. If you relink to a different Kalshi account, a 30-day cooldown applies before you can relink again. Free days and free weeks are tied to the linked pair, so they can't be passed to another account.

Is the 84.4% record real money?

No — it's a forward paper record: real live calls made in real time, graded against Kalshi's official settlements, traded with paper money. It's the honest way to prove a strategy before anyone's real account is involved, and it's labeled paper everywhere it appears.
